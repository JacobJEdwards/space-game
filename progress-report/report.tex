    \section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}

    Space Game is a 3D space shooter game developed using Unity Engine\citep{unity:home} and C\#\citep{csharp:home}.
    The game is set in a fictional solar system, that will eventually feature procedurally generated planets and
    asteroids, as well as a variety of enemies, NPCs and obstacles.

    The game is inspired by games such as No Man's Sky\citep{hellogames:nms} and Elite Dangerous\citep{frontier:ed},
    but with a low poly art style and a focus on exploration and combat.

    The target audience for the game is players who enjoy exploration and combat games, as well as players who enjoy
    games with a focus on upgrading and customising their equipment.

    The story of the game is that the player was travelling home through hyperspace, but their ship broke down under
    mysteries circumstances, and they are now stranded in a solar system with no way to contact anyone.
    The player must explore the solar system, find resources to repair their ship, and eventually find a way to
    escape, while meeting aliens, both friendly and hostile, and upgrading their ship and equipment along the way.

    \section{Background / Similar games}\label{sec:background}

    \subsection{No Man's Sky}\label{subsec:nms}

    No Man's Sky is a game developed by Hello Games\citep{hellogames:nms}, released in 2016.
    The game is set in a procedurally generated universe, with 18 quintillion planets to explore.
    The game features a variety of gameplay elements, such as exploration, combat, trading, survival and
    multiplayer, as well as features such as base building and farming.

    The game was met with a mixed reception at launch, with criticism of the lack of multiplayer, the lack of variety
    in the planets, and the lack of depth in the gameplay.
    However, the game has since been updated with a number of patches and expansions, adding new features and improving
    the gameplay experience.

    This game takes inspiration from No Man's Sky in terms of the exploration and variety of planets, as well as the
    focus on upgrading and customising equipment, but has a more fixed and focused story and a lower poly art style.

    \subsection{Elite Dangerous}\label{subsec:ed}

    Elite Dangerous is a game developed by Frontier Developments\citep{frontier:ed}, released in 2014.
    The game is set in a 1:1 scale replica of the Milky Way galaxy, with a variety of star systems to explore.
    The game features a variety of gameplay elements, such as exploration, combat, trading, mining and piracy, as well
    as features such as missions, factions and powerplay.

    I like the realism and scale of Elite Dangerous, and have taken inspiration from the game in terms of the space
    movement and combat mechanics, as well as the focus on exploration.

    \section{Design}\label{sec:design}

    The game is designed to be a 3D space shooter, with a focus on exploration and combat and a low poly art style.

    The majority of the assets are taken from the Unity Asset Store\citep{unity:assetstore}, and either adapted to fit
    the game's style, for example through changing the colours or generating animations, or used as is.

    I wanted a low poly art style, as I feel it is easier to create assets in this style, and it fits the space theme
    well, as well as being more performant than a more realistic style.

    Initially, I wanted to allow travelling to different star systems, but have since decided to focus on a single solar
    system, after discussions about the scope of the project.
